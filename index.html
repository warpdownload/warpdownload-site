WarpFetch for Mac

Download files from direct links, pause and resume supported transfers, and manage your queue. WarpFetch also supports FTP, FTPS and SFTP servers.

Get WarpFetch on the Mac App Store · macOS 14 or later.

Version 2.3 adds three free, successfully completed downloads across all supported sources before you choose a plan. Failed or canceled transfers do not count, and the separate seven-day introductory trial starts only after an eligible user confirms the yearly plan in the App Store.
