WarpFetch release notes

2.3 — November 11, 2026

Complete three downloads for free before choosing a plan. Manual downloads, supported browser handoffs from Safari or Chrome, and other supported sources share the same three completions. Failed or canceled transfers do not use one.

After the third saved file, WarpFetch presents the Pro options. This does not start a subscription or charge you. The yearly plan’s separate seven-day introductory trial begins only when an eligible user confirms the purchase through the App Store.

Version 2.3 also improves browser handoff feedback so the extension can explain when the free allowance is in use or completed.

View WarpFetch on the Mac App Store.

2.2 — September 27, 2026

This release fixes an issue with the Safari extension and includes minor bug fixes.
